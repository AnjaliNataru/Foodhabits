BASIC NUTRITION

Know your nutrients

🍚 Carbohydrates

Provide energy. Sources include rice, wheat, millets and fruits.

🥚 Protein

Helps build and maintain body tissues. Sources include eggs, pulses, milk and beans.

🥑 Fats

Provide energy and help the body absorb certain vitamins.

🥦 Fibre

Found in fruits, vegetables, pulses and whole grains.

🍊 Vitamins

Support many normal body functions and are found in varied foods.

🥛 Minerals

Important for bones, blood and other body functions.
